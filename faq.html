Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the platform.

Do I need a computer to use Bill With Me?

No, you can run your entire business from your smartphone. However, we also support desktops and tablets seamlessly.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all your shop data is encrypted and securely stored in the cloud. You are the only one who can access your customers and inventory.

Does it support GST billing?

Absolutely. You can generate both GST and non-GST bills, and export proper reports for your CA.

How does WhatsApp sharing work?

Once a bill is generated, click the 'Share to WhatsApp' button. It automatically formats the bill and opens WhatsApp with the customer's number.
